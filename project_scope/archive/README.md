# Archived plans

Completed or superseded plan docs (types 3–5 in `documentation/documentation_model.md`) move here once their work has merged and a successor phase is underway.

**Why keep them instead of deleting:** they are part of the portfolio's provenance — they show how the work was scoped and sequenced — and this mirrors the ADR-immutability ethic. Moving them out of `project_scope/` (rather than deleting) means a reader of the *live* `project_scope/` is never misled by a plan that no longer reflects current intent.

A doc in here is frozen as-of its archival; do not edit it to reflect later reality. If its subject matter is revived, write a new plan.
